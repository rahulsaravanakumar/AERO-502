Record the training meeting with all required faces visible and keep real credentials out of the recording. Trainees follow the user guide (**Help** in the website header, or `docs/USER_GUIDE.md`).

## Member teach-back

1. Trainee signs in with a practice member account.
2. Trainee opens **My Tasks** and explains why other members' work is absent.
3. Trainee changes an assigned task from Backlog to In progress.
4. Trainee records 1 hour and confirms the member and task totals.
5. Trainer asks the trainee to enter letters as hours.
6. Trainee uses the visible validation guidance to correct the value without developer help.
7. Trainee teaches the same workflow to another practice member.

## Chief Engineer maintenance teach-back

1. Trainee identifies the deployed commit and health endpoint.
2. Trainee follows the numbered update procedure in `ADMIN_GUIDE.md`.
3. Trainer presents a simulated failed release on the practice app.
4. Trainee locates the last known-good release and explains the rollback.
5. Trainee identifies the pre-release backup and explains when a restore is necessary.
6. Trainee teaches the procedure to another authorized maintainer.

## Evidence record

Record the date, application version, participants, practice app, observed result, questions, and follow-up decision. Do not record real passwords, API keys, or private member records.
